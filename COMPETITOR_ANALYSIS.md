# Competitor analysis

Existing blockers such as uBlock Origin, Privacy Badger, Ghostery, and browser-native protections already provide mature request blocking or tracker reduction. Advertising-profile viewers and projects such as Overprofiling focus on exposing portions of platform data. SOVRIVA does not claim to be the first blocker or first profile viewer.

## Specific gap addressed

SOVRIVA combines source-preserving advertising-profile imports with three original workflow layers: **Profile Drift Intelligence** distinguishes supported removal from incomplete exports; **Evidence Provenance** links each conclusion to its source record and uncertainty; and **Privacy Action Verification** compares a user-recorded action with a subsequent export without claiming internal platform deletion. Cross-platform reconciliation keeps ambiguous labels unmatched.
